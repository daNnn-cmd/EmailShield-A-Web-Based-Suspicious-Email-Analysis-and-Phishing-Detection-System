💡 What is Business Email Compromise (BEC) & Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where malicious actors deceive victims into handing over login credentials, sensitive data, or transferring organizational funds. Business Email Compromise (BEC) impersonates trusted executives, colleagues, or vendors to fraudulently redirect wire transfers.

🚨 1. Artificial Urgency & Coercive Deadlines

Phrases like "Account suspended in 24 hours", "Immediate action required", or "Strictly confidential, do not call" are engineered to create panic and bypass rational checks.

💳 2. Financial Changes & Wire Transfer Requests

Any email asking to update vendor banking details, switch routing numbers, or purchase gift cards should be flagged immediately. Always verify independently through a secondary verified communication channel.

🔗 3. Suspicious Links & Spoofed Domains

Hover before clicking! Attackers create deceptive lookalike domains (e.g., micros0ft.com or raw IP links like http://192.168.1.50/login) to steal your username and password.

📎 4. High-Risk Attachment Extensions

Never open attachments ending in .exe, .scr, .vbs, .bat, or double extensions like Document.pdf.exe. Avoid enabling macros in unknown Office files.
